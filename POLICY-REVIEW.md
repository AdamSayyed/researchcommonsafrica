# Privacy policy


Last updated: November 17, 2026


Research Commons Africa connects graduate students, researchers and industry professionals. For privacy questions or account requests, contact maryjoasisfoundation@gmail.com.


## Information we use


When you sign in with Google, Google shares your account identifier, email address and basic profile information, such as your name and profile image, with our authentication service. We use this information to authenticate you and manage your account. This sign-in does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Drive files or calendar.


We store the profile details you submit: name, career stage, affiliation, research field, city and country, research biography, and whether you are open to mentorship or collaboration. These details are accessible to other verified members. Your sign-in email is not displayed in the member directory. Information you put in your biography may also be visible to members.


## Service providers and storage


We use Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication and database storage, and Vercel to host the website. These providers process information needed to operate their services. Authentication cookies keep you signed in. Technical logs may include IP addresses, request details, timestamps and authentication events for operation, security and troubleshooting. Data may be processed outside your country.


## Your choices


You can edit your profile in the app and sign out. To request account deletion or ask about your information, email the contact above from your account email. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Removing the app’s access in your Google account stops that authorization but does not itself delete your Research Commons Africa profile.


## Retention and updates


Account and profile information remains stored until it is removed. Deleting active records may not immediately remove copies in provider backups or security logs, which follow the providers’ retention processes. We will update this page as the platform’s data practices change.

# Terms of use


Last updated: November 17, 2026


These terms describe use of Research Commons Africa, a community for research networking, mentorship and collaboration. Questions can be sent to maryjoasisfoundation@gmail.com.


## Your account and participation


Use an account you control and provide accurate professional information. Treat other members respectfully. Do not impersonate others, harass members, send spam, attempt unauthorized access, or upload information you do not have permission to share. Do not include confidential research or personal information about other people in your profile without permission.


## Profiles and contributions


You remain responsible for the information you submit. By saving a profile, you permit the platform to store and display it to verified members for networking. You retain ownership of your content. Any research collaboration, intellectual-property arrangement or mentorship agreement should be agreed directly between the participants.


## Service scope


The platform is an early-stage service. Features may change or be temporarily unavailable. A member profile does not establish that the platform has independently checked a person’s qualifications. Participation does not guarantee mentorship, funding, research results or collaboration.


## Privacy and account requests


Our privacy policy explains how account and profile information is used. Contact us to report misuse or request account deletion. We may restrict access in response to misuse or security concerns.
